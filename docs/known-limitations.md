# Known limitations

- All examples use synthetic data. They are intentionally smaller than the private operational repository.
- Preparation orientation is not an admission or eligibility decision.
- AI coding agents assisted implementation extensively. This is not a claim of entirely manual development or a proprietary model.
- Human interpretation, visual review and acceptance remain part of the operating model.
- Intake storage is in memory, with no auth, persistence, concurrent writes or production integrations.
- Research is an offline fixture exercise. It makes no live search or outreach calls.
- Editorial traceability is demonstrated against fictional specifications, not real university policy. The example is English only.
- Payments, email and analytics are outside the executable demos.
- Publication scanning checks defined patterns and known file formats. It cannot guarantee discovery of every secret or sensitive statement.
- Font files are pinned by checksums; Chrome and Poppler versions can affect rendering. Regeneration need not be byte-identical.
- This repository makes no claim about revenue, customer outcomes or product-market fit.
