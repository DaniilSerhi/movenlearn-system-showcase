# Research workflow

```text
Question
→ source discovery
→ page reading
→ evidence capture
→ structured handoff
```

The public example uses [local fictional sources](../demo/research/README.md). Discovery candidates include a relevant specification and an out-of-scope fixture. The handoff records what was accepted and why, then links those findings to the editorial ledger.

The private working model uses Agent-Reach tooling with Exa/mcporter and Jina for public source discovery and reading. MoveNLearn's contribution is the scoped integration and handoff discipline. [Agent-Reach belongs to its upstream project](https://github.com/Panniantong/Agent-Reach).

The public demo makes no live calls and needs no credentials. It does not prove search coverage, current provider availability or access to private channel histories. A published contact route would not establish authority or consent to receive outreach; no prospect records appear here.
