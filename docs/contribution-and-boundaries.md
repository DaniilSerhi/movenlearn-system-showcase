# Contribution and boundaries

Daniil Serhieiev defines the product logic, workflow requirements, information architecture, review criteria and operational boundaries. Implementation and iteration are carried out extensively with AI coding agents and existing third-party tools, with outputs reviewed against the defined requirements.

The contribution demonstrated here is the ability to turn a business process into explicit inputs, transitions, outputs and checks. It includes deciding where automation should stop and a person should take over.

This statement does not claim that all production code was handwritten, that Daniil developed Hallmark or Agent-Reach, or that MoveNLearn owns Stripe, MailerLite or an AI model.

The demonstrations are new, reduced examples based on a described operating model. They are not exports of private customer records or production source. Passing their tests does not certify the private application's behavior.
