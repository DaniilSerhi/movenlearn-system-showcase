# Editorial QA checklist

This checklist describes review of the included synthetic article, not a production approval.

| Gate | Acceptance criterion | This sample |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intent | Answers the bounded review-request question | Article stays within the demo |
| Sources | Every behavioral claim maps to C1-C5 | Local source-section links included |
| Unsupported claims | R1 is excluded from article copy | No outcome guarantee |
| Scope | Fictional source is visibly identified | Synthetic marker and fictional target |
| Conversion | One relevant local next step | Inspect the CRM record |
| Prose | No empty intro, fake expertise, stuffing or filler | Concise process explanation |
| Locales | Do not imply multilingual coverage | English-only limitation documented |
| Links | Targets and source fragments resolve | Checked by the local link verifier |

A human accepting a new article must read its sources and assess the wording. Link validation only proves that a target exists, not that it supports a claim.
